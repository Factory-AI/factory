Factory organizations have three membership roles: **User**, **Manager**, and **Owner**. Each role includes the permissions of the roles below it.

## At a glance

| Role    | Best for                                                   | Access level             |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| User    | Individual contributors using Factory day to day           | Standard product access  |
| Manager | Team admins who manage people, integrations, and usage     | Administrative access    |
| Owner   | Business or IT owners responsible for billing and identity | Full organization access |

## User

Users can access Factory and use the core product experience. This role is intended for members who need to create and work with Droid sessions, use their personal workspace, and view information that is relevant to their own activity.

Users can:

- Use Factory and Droid.
- View their own analytics and activity.
- Manage their own personal settings and connections.
- View other members when the organization allows member visibility.

Users cannot manage organization-wide settings, billing, members, or shared administrative controls.

## Manager

Managers can do everything Users can do, plus manage most day-to-day organization administration. This role is intended for team leads, workspace admins, or operations owners who need to keep the organization running.

Managers can:

- Invite members.
- Update member roles within allowed limits.
- Remove members.
- Manage organization integrations and repositories.
- View organization-level analytics and usage.
- Manage usage limits and usage-related controls.
- Manage organization settings and enterprise controls.
- Manage service accounts and shared computer resources.
- Purchase or configure extra usage where available.

Managers cannot manage owner-only billing, payment, or identity-administration actions.

## Owner

Owners can do everything Managers can do, plus access the most sensitive organization controls. This role is intended for the person or team ultimately responsible for the Factory account.

Owners can:

- Manage billing details and payment methods.
- Access the billing portal.
- Start or change high-impact subscription settings.
- Configure SSO, domain verification, and directory sync where available.
- Complete account onboarding or plan setup steps that require account ownership.
- Manage owner-only usage and billing controls.

## Notes

Exact access can vary based on your organization's plan, enabled features, and administrative settings. When in doubt, assign the lowest role that gives a member the access they need.
